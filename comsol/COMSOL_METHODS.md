## 1. Laminate mechanics (`mos2_patch_mechanics.mph`)

**Interfaces.** Solid Mechanics (2D, plane strain, linear elastic, geometrically linear) for all layers thicker than 1 um (PI, PET, PMMA, PDMS, rigid island). The nanometre layers are not meshed as solids. The bonded Au (50 nm), MoS2 (0.65 nm) and Al2O3 (5 nm) films are plane-strain membranes on the device boundary, added as Weak Contributions to Solid Mechanics: -E t/(1-nu^2) (du/dx)_T test((du/dx)_T). This is the membrane (Layered Shell membrane-only) formulation. COMSOL 6.0 has no Membrane or Layered Shell interface for 2D planar components, so the membrane is written directly as a boundary weak form.

**MoS2 channel and van der Waals transfer.** The channel (length L_ch = 10 um) carries its own axial displacement u_m on the boundary (Weak Form Boundary PDE) with membrane stiffness (E t)_MoS2 + (E t)_Al2O3. It is attached to the substrate surface at MoS2 mid-plane height, u_s = u - h_m dv/dx with h_m = t_Au + t_MoS2/2, through a tangential Thin Elastic Layer: -k_int (u_m - u_s) test(u_m - u_s). This is the Solid Mechanics Thin Elastic Layer equation (spring constant per unit area). It is written in weak form because the flake is a boundary, not a solid domain. With a free-ended flake the transferred strain follows shear-lag. eta is defined as the channel-averaged MoS2 strain divided by the strain of a perfectly bonded flake (pointwise constraint u_m = u_s), and k_int is calibrated by a secant iteration on COMSOL solves. Polymer and buried caps (eta = 1) use the pointwise constraint.

**Loading.** Pure bending: Rigid Connectors on both end faces with prescribed rotations +-kappa L/2, u_y = 0 on both ends and u_x = 0 on the left end only. The axial force is free, so N = 0 and the neutral plane is an outcome of the solve. Linear analysis at kappa_ref = 1 1/m, with an auxiliary sweep over the motion curvatures (1/25, 1/40, 1/300, 1/30 mm^-1). Outputs are read at x = 0, 5 mm from the loaded ends (Saint-Venant).

**Geometry variants.** Component `base`: substrate thickness and modulus are parameters (25 um PI baseline, 12.5 um PI, 2.5 um PET). Component `capped`: solid cap with thickness and modulus as parameters (1 um PMMA over 5 nm Al2O3; buried PI/PI channel with a 23 um top PI, giving about 1 um offset from the neutral plane; symmetric 25/25 um check). Component `island`: 3 mm x 20 um island (E = 70 GPa) embedded flush in a 100 um PDMS carrier (E = 2 MPa) under the 25 um PI device film. Prebent channel: no reasonable 2D geometry reproduces an out-of-plane prebuckled channel, so the published strain ratio (0.001) is applied to the COMSOL baseline.

**Elements and mesh.** Mapped quadrilateral mesh, quadratic serendipity displacement elements (Solid Mechanics default), quadratic Lagrange elements for u_m. Horizontal element size is graded: 0.2 um/m within 2 um of the flake ends, 1 um/m on the flake and up to 20 um from it, 10 um/m to 200 um, 50 um/m to 1 mm and 150 um/m to the strip end (5 um/m near the island edges). Through the substrate there are 2m elements, where m = 2^(level-1).

## 2. Sensor chain (`mos2_sensor_chain.mph`)

**Coupling to mechanics.** The laminate is linear and quasi-static, so eps_ch(t) = (eps/kappa) kappa(t) exactly. Instead of 36 000 structural solves, eps/kappa is taken from model 1 (COMSOL value per structure, parameter `epk`), and the chain runs as global ODE/DAE equations. This is the fallback the method plan allows.

**Global ODE** (Global Equations interface): d theta/dt = k_on C_b(t) (1 - theta) - k_off theta, theta(0) = 0.

**Global variables (exact COMSOL expressions):**

**Studies.** `std_stat`: stationary with an auxiliary sweep (four motions, seven structures, V_ov 0.05-1 V, r_c 0-0.8, mismatch 0-20 %, concentration and strain grids). `std_time`: 0-1800 s, output every 0.05 s (36 000 points), BDF with strict steps at output times, max step 0.05 s, rtol 1e-7. `std_motion`: 0-4 s waveforms.

**Post-processing** (`comsol/assemble.py`, on exported COMSOL time series): 2nd-order zero-phase Butterworth low-pass (scipy sosfiltfilt, f_c = 10 mHz, fs = 20 Hz), twin-sensor subtraction with 5 % gauge mismatch, inversion through the strain-free calibration curve, and per-block RMS and max error against the ideal motion-free reading. The filter-vs-frequency sweep (1 mHz-3 Hz) uses the COMSOL strain-to-current transfer curve (4001-point sweep, cubic interpolation).
